# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people,
and keep discussions on-topic. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer
([@RanveerMeel](https://github.com/RanveerMeel)). Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and restrict participation
of anyone who violates this code.
